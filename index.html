PriceLink

An eco-friendly IoT solution using E-Ink technology to eliminate retail pricing errors and paper waste.

Power Optimized

Sub-10µA deep-sleep logic ensuring a 3-4 year battery life for ESP32-based tags.

Real-Time Sync

Event-driven architecture with MQTT v5.0 and WebSockets for instant updates.

Data Integrity

100% data integrity with a bi-directional Acknowledgment (ACK) handshake protocol.
